Abstract: Recent pulsar timing array (PTA) results have provided evidence for the presence of a nanohertz gravitational wave (GW) background, most likely originating from a population of supermassive black hole binaries (SMBHBs). The next major milestone is the detection of continuous GWs (CGWs) from an individual SMBHB and the identification of its electromagnetic counterpart. Typically, searches for CGWs in PTA data assume binaries in circular orbits.
